Treatments

Fertility care for every situation

+

IVF / ICSI

A single sperm is injected into each egg, then the embryos are cultured and transferred.

+

Genetic testing (PGT)

PGT-A, PGT-SR and PGT-M check embryos for chromosomal and single-gene conditions.

+

Egg & embryo freezing

Preserve eggs, sperm or embryos at -196°C for the future.

+

EmbryoScope Plus

Time-lapse incubator with AI-assisted embryo assessment, 24 hours a day.

+

ERA test

Finds the best day for embryo transfer.

+

Male fertility

Semen analysis, PESA and TESE sperm retrieval.

+

IUI

Prepared sperm placed directly in the uterus.

+

PRP & carrier screening

Ovarian PRP and genetic screening for both partners.
