Since **1 May 2025**, the paper arrival card (TM6) has been replaced by the **Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)**. It is mandatory for every non-Thai traveller entering by air, land or sea — including children — and it remains in force under the new 30-day visa-exemption rules.

> **Use only the official website: [tdac.immigration.go.th](https://tdac.immigration.go.th). The TDAC is free.** Several look-alike websites charge "service fees" — you don't need them.

## When to fill it in

Submit the TDAC **within the three days (72 hours) before you arrive** in Thailand. You can still complete it later, up until you reach the immigration counter, but doing it at home saves stress at the airport.

## What you need

- Your **passport**
- Your **flight details** (flight number and arrival date)
- Your **address in Thailand** (hotel, hospital or apartment)
- An **email address** to receive the confirmation

Depending on your nationality and recent travel, the form may also ask a few **health declaration** questions.

## Step by step

1. Open **tdac.immigration.go.th** and choose to submit an arrival card.
2. Enter your personal details exactly as they appear in your passport.
3. Add your trip information: arrival date, flight number, purpose of visit and where you will stay.
4. Answer the health questions if they appear.
5. Review everything and submit.
6. Save the **confirmation email or PDF with the QR code** on your phone, or print it.

On arrival, show the QR code with your passport at immigration. If you didn't receive the email or QR code, tell the immigration officer.

## Good to know

- **One TDAC per entry.** If you leave Thailand and come back, you need a new one.
- **The TDAC is not a visa.** It does not replace a visa if your nationality or purpose of travel requires one.
- **Transit passengers** who do not pass through immigration don't need a TDAC.
- **Forgot?** Kiosks are available at Suvarnabhumi (BKK), Don Mueang (DMK), Phuket (HKT), Chiang Mai (CNX) and Hat Yai (HDY) airports.
